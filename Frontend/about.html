Why We Built BookSwap

New textbooks cost thousands of rupees, and the shops near campus rarely have the edition your teacher asked for. Meanwhile, the senior sitting next to you in the library has that exact book and no use for it anymore.

We are a team of five CS students who built BookSwap for our Software Project Management course. We wanted one place inside the department where those two students can find each other, agree on a price or a swap, and meet on campus.

What You Can Count On

  • Books For Your Courses

    Listings are sorted by CS core course, from Programming Fundamentals to Operating Systems, so you find the right book fast.

  • Talk Before You Pay

    Chat with the seller first. Ask about highlights or missing pages, and see the book in person on campus.

  • Safe Payments

    Pay with JazzCash or Easypaisa. The seller gets the money only after you confirm you have the book.
