Quick Answers

When does the seller get paid?

After you confirm on BookSwap that you have the book. Until then, your JazzCash or Easypaisa payment is held safely.

The book is not as described. What now?

Do not confirm the order. Send us a message with the book title and what is wrong, and we will look into it.

Can I cancel a swap?

Yes, until both of you confirm the swap. Just tell the other student in the chat and cancel it from your orders.
