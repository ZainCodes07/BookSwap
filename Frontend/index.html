How BookSwap Works

  1. 1

    Find Your Book

    Search by title or open a subject. Every listing shows the condition and the seller's semester.

  2. 2

    Chat With The Seller

    Ask about highlights or missing pages, agree on a price or a swap, and pick a meeting spot on campus.

  3. 3

    Pay Or Swap

    Pay through JazzCash or Easypaisa, or hand over your old book. The seller is paid once you confirm.
